# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.

## Mobile app decisions (`baitalebdaa_app/`)

- 18 Oct 2026 (replaces the earlier 4-tab layout): bottom navigation is Home · Services · Projects · Book · Profile, with Book as the strongest action. The AI assistant ("Ask Ali", the website assistant's name) is reached from a Home card, not a tab.
- 18 Oct 2026 (replaces splash → sign-in → Home): launch is splash → Home. No sign-in screen at launch; guests browse everything and can fill in a request.
- 18 Oct 2026: sending a consultation request requires signing in (at the Review step; the draft is kept while they sign in).
- 18 Oct 2026: Firebase is on the free Spark plan, so there is no Firebase Storage. Photos and plans are shared on WhatsApp after sending, with the request reference prefilled.
- 18 Oct 2026: Firebase project `baitalebdaa-76046` serves dev and staging; `baitalebdaa` is reserved for production so real customer data never mixes with test data.
- 20 Oct 2026: the Play package name is `com.baitalebdaa.app` (final; Play never allows a change). The production build uses the Firebase project `baitalebdaa`.
- 20 Oct 2026: every screen uses the one shared header in `lib/design_system/components/navigation/app_header.dart`: `AppScrollView` (eyebrow, large title, one line under it, collapsing into a slim pinned bar) for scrolling pages, `AppPhotoSliverHeader` for project pages, `AppTopBar` otherwise (service pages too since the 26 Oct design). Round 40 px header buttons; back appears only on pushed screens. No raw `AppBar`/`SliverAppBar` (the conventions check enforces it).
- 21 Oct 2026: icons are Iconsax (owner's choice), from the `iconsax_flutter` package, all through `lib/design_system/tokens/app_icons.dart`: linear everywhere, bold for the selected tab. Not the `iconsax` 0.0.8 package: it puts 3,424 glyphs on real Unicode letters (bold `user5` is Arabic U+0630 and renders blank) and one at code point 0, which breaks icon tree shaking.
- 21 Oct 2026: list cards and detail-page header photos are square (1:1); the website photos are all portrait (3:4 to 4:5), and landscape frames cut too much.
- 26 Oct 2026 (owner's design screenshots; replaces the ink look, the round header buttons and the ink circle on Book): the app is deep green `#1F4A3D` (dark theme `#8FC4AA`) with Cormorant Garamond for large Latin titles, page and bar titles (Arabic keeps Tajawal). The tab bar is plain icons; the selected tab is green and bold with a short line under its label, and Book is no longer emphasised. Header buttons are plain icons (dark glass only on photos), and back is an arrow.
- 26 Oct 2026: the redesigned screens are Home, Projects, Service detail and the request ("Plan Your Project"). They use only real data: the website's 6 projects and photos, the studio facts, and the four process stages; no invented projects, claims or photos. Heart (projects) and bookmark (services) save on the device and show in Profile > Saved. Service pages share their website page. Home's "UAE" picker prefills the request's emirate. Search covers services and projects.
- 26 Oct 2026: the request keeps its questions and data, grouped into screens (Requirements, Details, Consultation, Contact, then Review and Complete) with a step tracker, numbered sections, photo and icon tiles, and Continue plus Save Draft. Photos and plans stay on WhatsApp after sending (owner, 26 Oct; no in-app upload or email for now).
- 26 Oct 2026 (owner's splash, log-in and sign-up designs): the splash is a darkened real project photo with the wordmark, "INTERIORS | FIT-OUT | JOINERY", a brass rule, "Designing exceptional spaces across the UAE." and a spinner, shown at least 1.4 s. Log in is a photo across the top under a rounded sheet; sign up is wordmark, rounded photo, large serif title, then name, email, phone (UAE flag) and password, and a terms checkbox linking the website's Terms and Privacy pages. Both offer Google and Apple through Firebase (each provider must be switched on in the Firebase console; Apple also needs an Apple Developer account). The design's gold house mark is not the real logo, so the real wordmark is used, and its rendered rooms are replaced by website photos. The sign-up phone is kept on the device only and prefills requests. Launch stays splash → Home; sign-in still opens only from Profile or at Send.
- The app is premium project discovery + consultation request + request tracking. Not a shop, marketplace or instant-booking app. Never "Booking confirmed"; a request is "received" until staff schedule it. Only real services, prices and projects from the website's own data.

## Website admin panel (`/admin`)

- 26 Oct 2026 (owner's design and choices): www.baitalebdaa.com/admin, built in `src/app/admin` with its own root layout (none of the public site's header, Ask Ali or analytics), `noindex` and blocked in `robots.txt`.
- Leads are in Firestore (project `baitalebdaa`). Website forms (all but the newsletter; careers since 27 Oct 2026, as "Job application", with the CV left in the email) and Ask Ali quotes are saved to `leads` as well as emailed; app requests stay in `requests`. Statuses are the app's `RequestStatus` values, so a status set in the panel shows in the app; booking a site visit stores `visitAt`.
- Sign-in is Firebase email/password, checked on the server, for emails in `ADMIN_EMAILS` only; it issues an httpOnly session cookie. Settings: `FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT`, `FIREBASE_WEB_API_KEY`, `ADMIN_EMAILS` (see `.env.example`).
- Version 1 is Dashboard, Leads & Inquiries, lead detail and Bookings / Site Visits, from real data only. The other menu items show "Soon": their content still lives in `src/data`. Visitor numbers stay in Google Analytics.
- `ADMIN_PREVIEW=1` with `next dev` shows made-up leads for design checks; production builds can never show them.
- 27 Oct 2026 (owner): the panel is for staff who read little, so it stays simple: simple English, only Home · Customers · Visits in the menu, big coloured buttons with a word and an icon (Call, WhatsApp, Open), customer cards instead of tables, tabs New · Called · Visit · Done · Cancelled, and one-tap status buttons ("Visit booked" asks for the date). No charts, percentages or "Soon" items.
- 27 Oct 2026 (owner): Settings edits the admin's own name, phone and address (adminProfiles/{uid}) and the business details the website shows: phone, WhatsApp, main and sales email, address and opening hours in English and Arabic (Firestore settings/business, defaults in src/data/business.js). Pages read them through useBusiness() / getBusiness(); saving clears the cache (revalidateTag("business")). Text written into articles, FAQs and legal pages still names the default number and is not edited from the panel.
- 6 Nov 2026 (owner): Google Analytics loads only after the visitor accepts cookies (Consent Mode "basic"): no GA download and no cookieless pings before that. The Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy say so. It took ~2 s of a phone's main thread on every first visit.
- 6 Nov 2026 (owner): every cost estimator (homepage, pricing page, service pages) uses only the published prices in `src/data/pricing.js` (`SERVICE_ESTIMATORS` in `src/lib/pricing.js`); no flat made-up rates. Services with no published price show "Quoted after a free site survey" instead of an estimate.
- 6 Nov 2026 (conversion review): one main action per screen. The homepage hero has a fixed headline ("Interior Design, Turnkey Fit-Out & Custom Joinery Across the UAE"), "Request a Quote" as the main button, "Discuss on WhatsApp" second, and the free site survey as a quieter link; only the photos rotate (pause button; no autoplay with reduced motion). Service pages lead with "Request a Quote" (their service preselected), WhatsApp second, call and site visit as links.
- 11 Nov 2026 (owner): the homepage hero's headline, text, buttons, site-survey link and proof points are centred (desktop and mobile); the slide controls and caption stay at the bottom, under the copy in the normal flow (never overlapping it). A small glass badge above the headline reads "Serving all seven emirates of the UAE".
- 11 Nov 2026 (owner): the hero's "Request a Quote" is no longer the gold gradient (too dull and old-fashioned): it is a white pill with the deep green `#1F4A3D` arrow in a round chip. The gold buttons elsewhere are unchanged.
- 11 Nov 2026 (owner): the "Four stages" process timeline (ProcessTimeline, on every page that uses it) is kept simple: four plain white cards with photo, small step number, title, text and the location as a quiet footer line. No dark glowing panel, gold line or nodes, gold icon tiles, outlined numerals or pills ("too much").
- 11 Nov 2026 (owner): on the homepage, "What happens next" (NextSteps) sits directly above the "Four stages" process section.
- 6 Nov 2026: the quote form asks four things (name, phone or WhatsApp with country code, service, emirate); email, size, budget and description are optional. Success shows only after `/api/contact` confirms. Site visits are "Request a free site visit", never "Book", until staff confirm a time. No response-time promise unless the owner approves one.
- 6 Nov 2026: analytics events follow `docs/conversion-measurement.md` (consent first, no personal data, `generate_lead` only after a received enquiry, WhatsApp/phone clicks separate).
- 27 Oct 2026 (owner): for a signed-in customer the quote form and the Contact page project form fill in name, email, phone and address from their account (/api/account/me; address lives on profiles/{uid}). The saved lead carries customerUid, so it shows under their My Requests and the panel marks it "Signed-in customer".
